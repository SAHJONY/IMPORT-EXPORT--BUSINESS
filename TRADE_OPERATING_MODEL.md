# SAHJONY Global Trade — Zero-Own-Capital Profit Operating Model

## Operating thesis
SAHJONY Global Trade operates as an international trade originator, broker, sourcing coordinator and trade facilitator. The default and required model is **zero SAHJONY own capital**: no inventory purchases, supplier deposits, bridge financing, principal balance-sheet funding or inventory/title exposure by default.

SAHJONY creates value by originating verified demand, qualifying counterparties, sourcing, structuring RFQs, comparing quotations, coordinating compliance/logistics/documentation, protecting its compensation and advancing transactions to authorized human closing.

## Canonical profit funnel
Qualified Buyer → Verified Requirement → Supplier Quote → $0-Capital Funding Structure → Fee Protection → Compliance → Human Commercial Approval → Contract → Execution → Invoice → Cash Collected

Each stage is distinct. A research lead is not a qualified buyer. A quotation is not a contract. A contract is not an invoice. An invoice is not cash collected.

## Zero-capital gate
A transaction cannot pass the commercial close gate until all of the following are true:
1. SAHJONY capital required = **$0**.
2. Funding source is identified and verified.
3. Payment instrument/structure is defined.
4. Buyer payment capability is verified to the appropriate level.
5. Supplier payment trigger is defined.
6. SAHJONY is not assuming principal risk.
7. SAHJONY is not assuming inventory/title risk.

Acceptable structures may include, where commercially and legally appropriate:
- buyer-funded procurement;
- irrevocable documentary letter of credit;
- buyer-funded escrow;
- documentary collection;
- supplier credit/open account elected by the supplier;
- third-party trade finance that does not use SAHJONY capital.

If a transaction requires SAHJONY to fund inventory, make a supplier deposit, bridge the payment cycle or take material principal/title exposure, the Zero-Capital Gate is **BLOCKED** unless the operating model is expressly changed by the owner in a separate decision.

## Fee-protection gate
SAHJONY must have a documented compensation mechanism before substantial protected value is released or the transaction advances to execution, as commercially appropriate.

Permitted structures, subject to contract and applicable law, include:
- buyer-paid sourcing fee;
- supplier-paid commission;
- success fee;
- commercial representation fee;
- disclosed transaction margin/spread where lawful and contractually appropriate.

The Fee Protection Gate requires:
1. fee model defined;
2. fee rate or amount defined where applicable;
3. protection status executed/signed/confirmed/approved;
4. fee payment trigger defined.

No deceptive hidden markup or payment diversion is permitted.

## Buyer readiness
A buyer is commercially ready only when the system has sufficient evidence of:
- real business requirement;
- approximate quantity/specification/destination/timeline;
- legal entity and authorized decision-maker/mandate;
- payment capability or credible funding/payment path;
- next commercial action.

## Supplier readiness
A supplier is commercially ready only when the system has sufficient evidence of:
- legal identity/KYB;
- capability to supply the requirement;
- usable quotation or commercial terms;
- specifications/documentation/certifications as applicable;
- MOQ/capacity/lead time;
- payment terms and supplier payment trigger;
- delivery/incoterm capability.

## Revenue evidence ladder
The system must track these independently:
1. **Pipeline notional** — possible transaction size; not revenue.
2. **Expected fee** — modeled commercial opportunity; not contracted.
3. **Contracted fee** — compensation contractually established; not collected.
4. **Invoiced fee** — invoice issued; not collected.
5. **Collected fee** — cash actually received and evidenced.

**Primary profit KPI: collected fee revenue.**

## Agent autonomy
AI agents may autonomously:
- research and capture public commercial opportunities;
- qualify buyers and suppliers from evidenced facts;
- create/update CRM records;
- request RFQs and missing commercial information;
- compare quotations and logistics options;
- calculate indicative economics;
- draft non-binding communications;
- maintain follow-up and next-action queues;
- rank opportunities by readiness, economics and risk;
- identify missing Zero-Capital, Fee Protection, buyer, supplier and compliance gates.

AI agents may not autonomously:
- sign or accept contracts;
- accept binding pricing;
- release or redirect payments;
- change bank instructions;
- declare KYC, sanctions, export-control, credit or legal approval;
- represent unverified certification, inventory, authority or product availability;
- agree to exclusivity or financing commitments;
- make legal admissions.

## Mandatory commercial close gates
Every material transaction must pass:
1. Buyer identity/authority and requirement verification.
2. Supplier identity/capability and usable quote verification.
3. Zero-Capital Gate.
4. Fee Protection Gate.
5. Economics and transaction structure review.
6. Compliance/KYB/KYC/sanctions/export-control review as applicable.
7. Authorized human approval for binding commercial commitments.
8. Closing/execution evidence.
9. Invoice evidence.
10. Cash-collection evidence.

## Daily operating KPIs
Track at minimum:
- new qualified buyers;
- verified buyer requirements;
- supplier quotations received;
- zero-capital PASS opportunities;
- fee-protected opportunities;
- close-ready opportunities;
- expected fee USD;
- contracted fee USD;
- invoiced fee USD;
- collected fee USD;
- overdue buyer/deal follow-ups.

## Work allocation
Default operating emphasis:
- 60% buyer acquisition and demand qualification;
- 20% supplier quotation and commercial matching;
- 10% fee/payment protection;
- 10% compliance and execution controls.

Do not add product-specific public positioning unless a future commercial strategy explicitly requires it. Public acquisition remains product-agnostic: buyer requirement, supplier capability and qualified commercial opportunity.

## Ranking model
Prioritize work using buyer readiness, supplier readiness, zero-capital readiness, fee protection, compliance, recurrence, expected economics and closability. High transaction notional alone does not create priority.

## Revenue integrity
Research opportunity ≠ qualified lead ≠ verified requirement ≠ supplier quote ≠ fee-protected negotiation ≠ contracted fee ≠ invoiced fee ≠ collected revenue.

The platform is commercially successful only when a repeatable sequence produces **cash collected** while preserving the zero-own-capital rule and governance controls.
