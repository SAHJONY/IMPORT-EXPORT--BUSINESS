# SAHJONY Global Trade — Capital-Light Trade Operating Model

## Operating thesis
SAHJONY Global Trade operates primarily as an international trade originator, broker, sourcing coordinator and trade facilitator. The default model is capital-light: do not purchase inventory or commit SAHJONY capital unless an owner-approved transaction structure specifically requires it and all applicable risk gates have passed.

## Core commercial funnel
Lead → AI Qualification → CRM → RFQ → Supplier → Quote → Compliance → Negotiation → Contract → Logistics → Revenue

Each stage must remain distinct. A lead is not a deal. A quotation is not contracted revenue. A contract is not collected revenue. Revenue is recognized only from appropriate ledger/evidence.

## Monetization
Permitted business-model options, subject to contract and applicable law:
- broker commission;
- sourcing fee;
- transaction margin;
- representation fee;
- distribution margin when explicitly authorized and commercially justified.

The system should prefer structures that minimize inventory exposure, working-capital needs, title risk and unsecured counterparty exposure.

## Commercial priorities
### Tier A — EV charging equipment
Primary execution priority. Convert verified supplier conversations into product qualification, quotations and buyer acquisition. Require verified model/catalog, certifications, MOQ/capacity, pricing, lead time, warranty/support, payment terms and delivery capability before presenting claims to buyers.

### Tier B — Industrial equipment
Primary execution priority. Originate buyer requirements and source qualified manufacturers/suppliers. Build quote comparisons, landed cost and commercial next steps.

### Tier C — Energy and commodities
High-value pipeline with elevated risk controls. Do not treat an inquiry, LOI, ICPO, SCO, mandate claim, proof-of-product claim or quotation as verified capability by itself. Require enhanced counterparty identity, authority, product/title evidence, sanctions/export-control screening, economics and human authorization before any binding step.

## Agent autonomy
AI agents may autonomously:
- research and capture public commercial opportunities;
- qualify buyers and suppliers;
- create/update CRM records from evidenced facts;
- request RFQs and missing commercial information;
- compare quotations and logistics options;
- calculate indicative landed-cost/economic scenarios;
- draft non-binding commercial communications;
- schedule follow-up and maintain next-action queues;
- classify opportunities by fit, urgency, value, probability, margin and risk.

AI agents may not autonomously:
- sign or accept contracts;
- accept binding pricing;
- release or redirect payments;
- change bank instructions;
- declare KYC, sanctions, export-control or credit approval;
- represent unverified certification, inventory, allocation, authority or product availability;
- agree to exclusivity or financing commitments;
- make legal admissions.

## Mandatory transaction gates
Every material transaction must pass:
1. Identity — verified legal entity/contact and authority.
2. Capability — real buyer demand or supplier capability supported by evidence.
3. Documentation — required product/corporate/commercial documents obtained.
4. Economics — pricing, fees/margin, logistics, landed cost and downside understood.
5. Compliance — applicable sanctions/export controls/KYB/KYC and regulatory checks.
6. Human authorization — required before any binding commitment.
7. Closing evidence — contract/payment/logistics status recorded distinctly.

## KPI targets
### 72 hours
- consolidate active RFQs;
- finish Servotech supplier qualification;
- qualify active buyers;
- remove invalid contacts;
- every opportunity has buyer/supplier/value/next action;
- validate inbound voice routing end-to-end.

### 7 days
- ≥25 qualified opportunities;
- ≥10 real RFQs;
- ≥5 verified quotations;
- ≥3 buyer/supplier matches;
- ≥1 advanced commercial negotiation.

### 30 days
- first commercial closing target;
- measurable pipeline economics;
- automated funnel operating from lead through revenue evidence.

## Ranking model
Prioritize work by a governed score derived from:
commercial fit × urgency × transaction value × probability of conversion × expected margin × strategic value, adjusted downward for verification gaps, counterparty risk, compliance risk and capital exposure.

## Revenue integrity
Research opportunity ≠ qualified lead ≠ quote ≠ negotiation ≠ contracted revenue ≠ collected revenue. The Owner OS must preserve these distinctions in every dashboard and agent action.
