# SAHJONY Managed Trade Gateway

## Operating position
SAHJONY is the managed-trade gateway between an eligible buyer/private business and the supplier market. The platform is not a passive directory. SAHJONY receives demand, sources and vets suppliers, structures the transaction, coordinates authorization/compliance, manages commercial/logistics/payment milestones, and controls final release.

Default legal role: `MANAGED_TRADE_ORCHESTRATOR`.

SAHJONY does **not** assume exporter-of-record, importer-of-record, principal, distributor, customs-broker, freight-forwarder, or financial-institution status merely by managing a transaction. Those roles must be explicitly assigned and evidenced per transaction.

## Managed flow
1. Private business/buyer submits a product need.
2. SAHJONY qualifies the request and verifies the private business where applicable.
3. SAHJONY sources candidate suppliers.
4. Supplier compliance, quality, commercial and bank due diligence are completed.
5. Owner selects the supplier for commitment.
6. Product classification and authorization scope are matched.
7. Quote, landed cost, margin and commercial terms are approved.
8. Exporter of record, importer of record, broker, forwarder and settlement provider are explicitly assigned.
9. Payment path, documentation, logistics and compliance gates pass.
10. Owner releases the managed case for execution.
11. SAHJONY monitors shipment, delivery and exceptions.
12. Final supplier/freight/duty/customer reconciliation and transaction P&L close the case.

## Control principle
No employee, supplier or customer can bypass the release workflow. Supplier selection requires compliance, quality and bank due diligence. Execution release requires all pre-delivery managed-trade milestones to PASS or be formally NOT_APPLICABLE, including compliance and owner release.

## Cuba private-business overlay
When destination is Cuba and a Cuban private business participates, the managed case must also pass the Cuba Private Business Desk and Cuba Authorized Trade Desk. Private-business status is not a substitute for product, authorization, end-user, sanctions, banking, document or logistics controls.
