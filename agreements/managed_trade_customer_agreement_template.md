# SAHJONY GLOBAL TRADE — MANAGED TRADE CUSTOMER AGREEMENT TEMPLATE

**Status:** DRAFT TEMPLATE — requires transaction-specific completion, appropriate legal review and signatures before use.

## 1. Parties

This Managed Trade Customer Agreement (the “Agreement”) is between **SAHJONY** (“SAHJONY”) and the customer identified in the transaction record (“Customer”).

## 2. Scope

Customer appoints SAHJONY to perform the services expressly selected for a transaction, which may include supplier sourcing, quote normalization, supplier due diligence coordination, commercial-term coordination, documentation workflow, logistics coordination, payment-process coordination, transaction monitoring and reconciliation.

SAHJONY’s exact legal/commercial role must be identified for each transaction. Unless expressly stated in a signed transaction schedule, SAHJONY acts as a managed-trade orchestrator/intermediary and does not automatically become seller, buyer, exporter of record, importer of record, customs broker, carrier, insurer, bank, escrow agent or regulated financial institution.

## 3. Transaction Schedule

Each transaction schedule must identify at minimum: product/specification; quantity; seller/supplier; buyer/customer; origin; destination; Incoterm; price/currency; SAHJONY fee/commission/margin; payer/payee; deposit/milestones/balance; shipping method; insurance responsibility; exporter of record; importer of record; customs broker; freight forwarder/carrier; authorization basis where required; and required evidence/release gates.

## 4. Compliance and Release

No commercial attractiveness, customer request, AI recommendation or prior course of dealing overrides applicable law or a required release gate. SAHJONY may place a transaction on HOLD when product classification, sanctions/restricted-party screening, end use/end user, banking/payment path, license/exception/authorization, customs, documents, insurance, logistics or other mandatory evidence is unresolved.

For Cuba-related transactions, private-sector eligibility is not itself authorization for a transaction. Product, parties, end use/end user, authorization basis, payment route and logistics must be assessed separately.

## 5. Supplier Selection

Supplier candidates are subject to due diligence. Customer understands that supplier selection does not eliminate commercial, quality, fraud, production, shipping, customs, political, banking or force-majeure risks. SAHJONY will not commit to a supplier until the required internal approvals are complete.

## 6. Payments

Payment instructions must identify the verified beneficiary and transaction purpose. Changes to beneficiary/bank details require independent re-verification. Customer acknowledges that SAHJONY will not represent that it holds funds in escrow or provides regulated payment services unless a separate lawful provider arrangement expressly supports that role.

## 7. Fees

SAHJONY’s compensation must be stated in the transaction schedule as a fixed fee, sourcing fee, management fee, percentage commission, success fee, disclosed margin, or other agreed structure. Any dual-side compensation or representation must be disclosed and approved where applicable.

## 8. Third-Party Providers

Customs brokers, freight forwarders, carriers, insurers, banks/payment providers, inspection companies, warehouses and other third parties remain independent parties responsible for their own regulated or contracted services.

## 9. Records and Audit

Customer authorizes SAHJONY to retain transaction records, due-diligence evidence, commercial communications, approvals, payment references and logistics documents as required for operations, compliance, accounting, dispute handling and audit, subject to applicable privacy and retention requirements.

## 10. No Guarantee

SAHJONY does not guarantee government authorization, customs release, supplier performance, financing, banking acceptance, delivery dates, duties/taxes, exchange rates or commercial outcome. Any estimate is subject to current facts and third-party conditions.

## 11. Governing Terms

Governing law, venue, limitation of liability, indemnification, confidentiality, dispute resolution, termination and any required notices must be completed in the signed master agreement or transaction schedule after legal review.
