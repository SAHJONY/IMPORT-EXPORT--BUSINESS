# SAHJONY GLOBAL TRADE — OPERATING PARTNER DUE DILIGENCE CHECKLIST

Use before approving any customs broker, freight forwarder, carrier, cargo insurer, payment provider, bank, accountant/tax advisor, inspection company, warehouse or 3PL.

## Identity and authority
- Legal name and DBA
- Entity jurisdiction and registration evidence
- Physical address and primary contacts
- Beneficial ownership/control where relevant
- Required professional/regulatory licenses
- License number and independent verification source
- Sanctions/restricted-party screen result

## Service scope
- Exact services SAHJONY may request
- Countries/corridors supported
- Commodity/product restrictions
- Cuba-specific restrictions or exclusions where relevant
- Subcontractors/agents used and responsibility allocation
- Service levels and escalation contacts

## Commercial terms
- Rate card / quote method
- Credit terms / deposit / prepayment requirements
- Currency and payment method
- Cancellation/claims rules
- Insurance requirements
- Liability limitations and indemnities

## Data and security
- Data shared with partner
- Access controls
- Privacy/security commitments
- Incident notification
- Record retention
- API/account credential handling where applicable

## Evidence required before APPROVED
- Signed agreement/engagement letter
- Current licenses/certificates
- W-9/W-8 or tax documentation as applicable
- Certificate of insurance where applicable
- Bank-beneficiary verification for payees
- Named operational contacts
- Successful sandbox/test transaction or production-safe E2E where possible
- Owner approval recorded in operating_partners

## Cuba corridor additional controls
- Written confirmation the partner can support the exact lawful transaction type
- Commodity/end-use restrictions documented
- Payment/banking restrictions documented
- Carrier/port/customs route documented
- Required licenses/authorizations identified
- No reliance on consumer parcel capability as proof of commercial authorization

No partner is APPROVED solely because it appears reputable, has good reviews, or markets international capabilities.
