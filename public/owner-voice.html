Governance

Inbound: Bland is the phone/SIP transport. Calls are forwarded to the configured OpenAI project SIP endpoint and must arrive as a realtime.call.incoming event before the backend accepts the call.
Verification: The platform reads Bland's live SIP configuration and checks the expected OpenAI project destination and TLS transport.
Approval gates: The phone agent cannot bind SAHJONY to pricing, contracts, payments, exclusivity, KYC, sanctions clearance, credit decisions or other legal/commercial commitments.
Recording: Intentional call recording remains disabled.
