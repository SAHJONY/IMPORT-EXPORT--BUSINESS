What SAHJONY does

One operating system from need to delivery.

We are not a passive supplier directory. SAHJONY structures and manages the transaction around the customer’s business requirement.

01 · SOURCE

Find suppliers globally

Search eligible origin countries and compare supplier quality, MOQ, price, lead time, terms and corridor viability.

02 · VERIFY

Qualify the trade

Screen parties, validate product classification and origin, and determine required customs, sanctions, banking and authorization controls.

03 · STRUCTURE

Build commercial terms

Normalize quotes, Incoterms, deposits, milestones, fees, insurance, freight, duties and SAHJONY compensation.

04 · EXECUTE

Coordinate the transaction

Manage documents, beneficiary verification, supplier milestones, freight handoff, customs coordination and shipment evidence.

05 · DELIVER

Track to receipt

Follow logistics milestones, exceptions, delivery and proof of receipt while preserving case-level controls.

06 · RECONCILE

Close the economics

Reconcile customer funds, supplier costs, freight, duties, insurance, refunds, claims, fees and final transaction P&L.
