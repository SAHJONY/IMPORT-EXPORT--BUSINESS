VERIFICATION IN PRACTICE

What we check before a transaction advances.

“Verified” is not binary. We maintain separate evidence states so a supplier can be real but its inventory unverified, or a quote can be real while buyer authority remains pending.

01 · IDENTITYWho is the counterparty?

Legal business name, business contact, domain/website where available, corporate documentation when required, and consistency across the submitted evidence.

02 · AUTHORITYCan this person act?

Role, mandate or decision authority is evaluated before sensitive introductions, commercial disclosure or binding steps.

03 · CAPABILITYCan they perform?

Product specifications, capacity, certifications, technical documentation, export experience, stock evidence or other category-specific support.

04 · COMMERCIALAre the terms executable?

Price basis, quantity, MOQ, Incoterm, lead time, validity, payment instrument and inconsistencies between buyer and supplier requirements.

05 · RISK & LOGISTICSWhat must clear first?

Applicable compliance checks, destination/origin constraints, inspection requirements, documentary controls and logistics assumptions.

06 · PAYMENT CONTROLWhat changes money?

Banking or beneficiary changes, payment release and other high-consequence actions require independent authorized review; they are not delegated to an AI agent.

DATA & PRIVACY

Public intake should not feel like a blind data handoff.

We ask for the minimum information needed to understand a commercial requirement. More sensitive evidence belongs later in the qualification process and should be requested only when it is relevant.

PUBLIC FORMS

What we ask for first

  • Business legal name and business contact.
  • Product or supply requirement, quantity, destination and specifications.
  • Optional commercial details such as target timing, Incoterm or company website.

Do not send passwords, card data, online-banking credentials, private keys, SSNs or unrelated personal identity documents through public intake forms.

LATER-STAGE EVIDENCE

Why additional documents may be requested

  • KYB or corporate documents when identity/authority needs stronger support.
  • Certifications, technical sheets, stock/capability evidence or inspection records where relevant.
  • Payment-instrument or banking evidence only within a controlled transaction workflow and only when commercially necessary.

Submission does not authorize public disclosure, create exclusivity or create a binding commercial commitment.

CREDIBILITY BY EXCLUSION
