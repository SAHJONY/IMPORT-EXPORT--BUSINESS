EVIDENCE-LED EXECUTION

Every stage has a different proof threshold.

A sourced company is not automatically a qualified buyer, a responsive supplier is not automatically verified, and an indicative price is not a firm quotation. SAHJONY TRADING OS keeps those states distinct.

01Research lead

Potential buyer or supplier identified from a legitimate source.

02Qualified demand

Buyer identity and actionable requirement confirmed.

03Firm quotation

Current supplier terms, validity, quantity and trade terms documented.

04Contracted trade

Authorized parties have entered a binding transaction.

05Invoice

Payment obligation formally issued under approved terms.

06Collected revenue

SAHJONY revenue is recognized only when legitimately received.

07Shipment

Released logistics execution is tracked against documentary evidence.

08Reconciliation

Delivery, economics and final records are closed against evidence.
