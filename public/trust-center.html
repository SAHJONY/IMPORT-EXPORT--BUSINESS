OPERATING MODEL

SAHJONY is a commercial coordinator, not an invisible claim generator.

The operating model combines a human-led trade desk with software and AI assistance. Technology expands sourcing coverage, organizes evidence, drafts non-binding communications, tracks follow-up and identifies missing inputs. Authorized humans retain control over material commercial commitments, payment actions and other high-consequence decisions.

COUNTERPARTY VERIFICATION

Verification is a chain of evidence, not a badge.

Different claims require different proof. A real legal entity may still have unverified stock. A valid quotation may still depend on unverified buyer authority. A certification PDF may still need issuer or scope confirmation. We therefore track separate states instead of collapsing everything into “verified / not verified.”

01 · SOURCED

Potential counterparty identified from a source or referral.

02 · CONTACTED

Business contact established or response received.

03 · DOCUMENTED

Relevant business/capability evidence received.

04 · COMMERCIAL

Current product/price/term information is available.

05 · RISK GATES

Required compliance, payment and logistics issues reviewed.

06 · APPROVED

Authorized human approval obtained for the next binding step.

BUYER / DEMAND SIDE

Typical evidence

  • Legal business identity and business contact.
  • Defined product, specifications, quantity and destination.
  • Decision authority or procurement role where relevant.
  • Commercial timeline, budget framework or payment instrument when needed.
  • Additional KYB or project evidence before sensitive introductions or high-value execution.
SUPPLIER / SUPPLY SIDE

Typical evidence

  • Legal company identity and responsive business contact.
  • Product/model/grade specifications and available capacity.
  • Current quotation or commercial basis.
  • Certifications/technical files when required by product or destination.
  • Stock, production, inspection or export evidence when the claim depends on it.
Identity evidenceDoes the legal entity and contact information consistently identify the same organization?
Authority evidenceDoes the person have a role or mandate appropriate to the requested commercial action?
Capability evidenceIs the supplier's product, capacity or asset claim supported by relevant current material?
Commercial evidenceAre price, quantity, Incoterm, validity, payment and lead time explicit enough to compare?
Risk evidenceAre category-, country- and transaction-specific controls addressed before commitment?
Execution evidenceAre shipment, delivery, payment and reconciliation milestones supported before revenue is treated as realized?

PRIVACY & DATA HANDLING

We separate initial commercial intake from sensitive transaction evidence.

A procurement manager or supplier should not have to submit unnecessary sensitive information merely to ask whether SAHJONY can help. Public forms are intended for business identity and requirement/capability information.

INITIAL PUBLIC INTAKE

Appropriate information

  • Business legal name, business contact and business email.
  • Product/supply requirement, quantity, destination, specifications and timing.
  • Company website and ordinary commercial notes.
  • Optional target budget, Incoterm or standard payment preference.

Do not submit through public forms: passwords, online-banking credentials, payment-card data, private keys, SSNs, unrelated passport scans or other credentials that can directly unlock an account.

QUALIFICATION / EXECUTION

Additional evidence only when needed

  • Corporate/KYB documents to support identity or authority.
  • Technical/certification documents to support product claims.
  • Inspection, inventory or production evidence where relevant.
  • Payment-instrument information only when the transaction reaches the stage where it is commercially necessary.

Data should be used for qualification, sourcing, coordination, audit and the related commercial relationship—not as a license to publish a counterparty's confidential information.

Data minimization principle: collect what is needed for the present stage, request stronger evidence only when the opportunity advances, and avoid placing reusable credentials or unrelated sensitive personal information into general-purpose notes.
COMMERCIAL CLAIMS POLICY

How SAHJONY distinguishes progress from outcome.

PIPELINE

Opportunity ≠ revenue

A potential margin or commission can be modeled for decision-making, but it remains projected until the underlying transaction completes and the relevant compensation is earned and reconciled.

QUOTE

Quote ≠ contract

A supplier quotation can be genuine while buyer acceptance, payment mechanics, intermediary protection or other conditions remain unresolved.

CONTRACT

Contract ≠ collection

A signed document does not by itself establish shipment, delivery, payment or collected revenue. Those later stages require their own evidence.
