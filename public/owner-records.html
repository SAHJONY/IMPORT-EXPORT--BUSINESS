Record Discipline

Rules that keep the CRM commercially useful.

NO DUPLICATESNormalize legal/business name and preserve registry references before creating a new record.
EVIDENCE FIRSTKeep source URL, provenance, verification date and public contact evidence attached to each record.
RESEARCH ≠ BUYERA registry listing is not an RFQ, buyer mandate or active demand signal.
CONNECT THE DEALWhen evidence exists, link the buyer/MIPYME to supplier, partner, deal and economics instead of duplicating the same company.
