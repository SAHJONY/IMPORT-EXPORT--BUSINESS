MANUFACTURERS · EXPORTERS · STAGED QUALIFICATION

Present supply with the evidence a buyer can trust.

Register export-ready capability for review and potential matching with real buyer requirements. Registration starts a qualification process; it does not automatically make a company or product “verified,” approved, exclusive or guaranteed to receive orders.

1 · IdentityLegal company identity and a responsive business contact.
2 · CapabilityProducts, models/grades, specifications, MOQ/capacity, lead time and target markets.
3 · EvidenceCertifications, technical files, stock/production support or export experience when relevant to the claim.
4 · Commercial fitCurrent quotation basis, Incoterms, payment terms and buyer/destination requirements must align before presentation.
What verification means: SAHJONY tracks identity, authority, capability, commercial and risk evidence separately. A supplier can be a legitimate company while current stock, capacity or certification scope remains pending. Public registration should not include passwords, bank-login credentials, card data, private keys or unrelated identity documents. See the verification standard.
