What should I send after I register?

Send the buyer or business name, WhatsApp/contact, product requested, approximate quantity, destination and any real purchasing requirement you know. If you manage a WhatsApp group or network of gestores, include the size of the group and the provinces or sectors you cover.

Compensation standard: commissions or referral compensation are not created merely by submitting a name. Eligible compensation depends on written terms, attribution, an earned event and applicable commercial/compliance review. Registry-only or speculative leads are not treated as closed business.
