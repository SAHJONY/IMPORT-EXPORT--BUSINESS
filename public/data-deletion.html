User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 5, 2026

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your interactions with SAHJONY LLC and SAHJONY Global Trade.

How to request deletion

  1. Email sahjonyllc@gmail.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request”.
  2. Identify the account, phone number, email address, or messaging profile associated with the information you want deleted.
  3. Provide enough information for us to reasonably verify that the request relates to you.

We will process valid requests subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, contractual, and record-retention requirements. Where information must be retained, we will limit retention to the applicable purpose and period.

Questions about deletion requests may be sent to sahjonyllc@gmail.com.
