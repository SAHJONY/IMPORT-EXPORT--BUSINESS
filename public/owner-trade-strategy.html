Hard governance gate

AI may research, qualify, create/update CRM records, request RFQs, compare quotes, calculate indicative economics and schedule follow-up. AI may not sign contracts, accept binding price, release/redirection payments, change bank instructions, declare KYC/sanctions/export-control approval, represent unverified certification/availability, accept exclusivity or make legal admissions.
