CONTROLLED WORKFLOW

From commercial need to reconciled delivery

01

Capture demand

Business need, quantity, specifications, budget, destination and target date.

02

Qualify business

Private-sector eligibility, counterparties, end user and end use are reviewed.

03

Source worldwide

Supplier candidates are compared for price, quality, MOQ, lead time and corridor fit.

04

Classify & control

Product classification, authorization scope, sanctions and required evidence are checked.

05

Structure terms

Commercial role, price, Incoterm, documents, responsibilities and economics are defined.

06

Validate payment

Beneficiary, bank, payment sequence and compliance evidence are verified.

07

Move goods

Broker, forwarder, carrier, insurance, customs and milestone tracking operate as one case.

08

Reconcile

Delivery is confirmed and supplier, freight, duty and SAHJONY economics are closed with evidence.
