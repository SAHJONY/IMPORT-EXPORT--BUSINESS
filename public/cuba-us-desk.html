OPERATIONAL RULE

Lead ≠ approval. Supplier ≠ authorization. Payment ≠ release.

The desk is built to preserve that separation. Customer demand can enter sourcing while the transaction remains on HOLD. Commercial work can continue, but shipment/payment release remains governed by evidence and Owner authorization.
