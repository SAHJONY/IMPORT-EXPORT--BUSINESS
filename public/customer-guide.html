CUSTOMER GUIDE · HOW THE BUSINESS OPERATES

How SAHJONY Global Trade works.

SAHJONY is an international sourcing and trade-facilitation business. We help qualified businesses define a real requirement, identify and qualify counterparties, manage RFQs and quotations, review commercial and compliance factors, coordinate negotiation, and move approved transactions toward execution. We do not require customers to understand the full international trade process before contacting us.

THE PROCESS

From business need to controlled execution.

01Submit a requirement

Tell us what your business needs, approximate quantity, destination, timing and contact information.

02Qualification

We clarify specifications, commercial requirements, decision authority and missing information.

03Counterparty sourcing

We identify potential suppliers, buyers or commercial counterparties that fit the requirement.

04RFQ and quotation

Comparable commercial terms are requested and organized without treating an unverified quote as a completed deal.

05Due diligence

Identity, capability, documentation, economics and applicable compliance issues are reviewed before a binding step.

06Negotiation

SAHJONY coordinates non-binding commercial discussion, clarifications, logistics assumptions and transaction structure.

07Human approval

Binding price, contract, payment, banking changes and regulated decisions require authorized human approval.

08Execution and closing

Once approved, documents, logistics and closing milestones are coordinated and revenue is recognized only from real evidence.

WHAT SAHJONY DOES

BUYER SIDE

Requirement & sourcing

Clarify demand, find potential supply, compare options and organize the commercial decision process.

SUPPLIER SIDE

Capability & market access

Qualify legitimate supply capabilities and match them against relevant business requirements when appropriate.

TRANSACTION

Trade coordination

Coordinate RFQs, quotations, commercial terms, documents, logistics and governed closing milestones.

Important: an inquiry is not a deal, a quotation is not a contract, and a contract is not collected revenue. SAHJONY separates each stage so customers know exactly what is verified and what still requires approval.

CUSTOMER PROTECTION

What the platform will not do automatically.

NO FALSE CERTAINTY

No invented availability or approval

We do not represent unverified inventory, capacity, certification, compliance status or counterparty authority as fact.

NO AUTONOMOUS COMMITMENT

Human control of binding actions

AI may qualify, organize and follow up, but cannot independently sign contracts, accept binding prices or release funds.

NO UNVERIFIED PAYMENT CHANGE

Payment and banking controls

Payment release, beneficiary changes and bank-instruction changes require authorized verification.

FAQ

What customers usually need to know.

Do I need to know the supplier already?

No. You can start with a defined business requirement and SAHJONY can coordinate supplier discovery and qualification.

Does submitting a request guarantee a quote?

No. Availability, pricing and commercial terms depend on verified counterparties and the actual requirement.

Can SAHJONY handle international coordination?

Yes, subject to the transaction, eligible countries, counterparties, documentation and applicable law.

When does a transaction become real?

Only after the relevant counterparties, commercial terms, documentation, compliance gates and authorized approvals are sufficiently verified.
