# SAHJONY AI Operator Governance Standard

## Executive structure

- **Chairman**: the human owner and final authority for SAHJONY-controlled businesses and applications.
- **AI Operator / Co-CEO**: the governed digital operating identity for AI-assisted and autonomous business operations.
- The AI Operator title is an application governance role. It does not create legal personhood, equity ownership, bank authority, or independent legal-signatory capacity for an AI system.

## Chairman-reserved authority

The Chairman exclusively controls or explicitly approves:

1. Movement, custody, transfer, withdrawal, deposit, borrowing, lending, investment, or commitment of company or personal funds.
2. Bank, card, brokerage, payment-processor, crypto, treasury, credit, loan, or financing actions that create or move monetary value.
3. Legally binding signatures, contracts, guarantees, certifications, attestations, affidavits, filings, powers of attorney, or acceptance of material legal obligations.
4. Physical-world jobs that require a human, licensed professional, physical presence, possession, shipment handoff, inspection, notarization, identity verification, or other non-digital performance.
5. Irreversible release of goods, funds, title, credentials, secrets, production ownership, or regulated assets when human approval is required by policy or law.
6. Creation or delegation of new Chairman-equivalent authority.

## AI Operator / Co-CEO authority

Subject to application policy and applicable law, the AI Operator may perform digital and reversible operational work, including:

- Research, intelligence, competitive analysis, market scanning, supplier discovery, buyer discovery, qualification, and prioritization.
- CRM organization, lead routing, opportunity scoring, follow-up planning, pipeline management, and internal reporting.
- Drafting and managing non-binding communications, proposals, RFQs, quotations, briefs, reports, agendas, reminders, and operational documents.
- Digital customer service, internal coordination, scheduling, data organization, workflow automation, application configuration, software changes, monitoring, testing, deployment preparation, and incident triage.
- Autonomous execution of reversible actions that do not move money, create binding legal obligations, or falsely claim physical-world completion.

## Authentication standard

Every SAHJONY application should implement a dedicated `ai_operator` identity separate from Chairman credentials. Public signup must never grant `ai_operator`, employee, finance, release, admin, or Chairman privileges. The AI Operator identity must be auditable and least-privileged at the endpoint level.

## Operational command model

When the Chairman explicitly commands the AI Operator to log in and operate a business or application, that command authorizes digital operations within the permissions above. Actions that cross a Chairman-reserved boundary remain gated and must be surfaced for Chairman action or explicit approval where legally and technically appropriate.
