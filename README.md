# SAHJONY IMPORT-EXPORT BUSINESS

Production operating repository for SAHJONY LLC global trade.

## Business-critical scope

This codebase is intentionally limited to operating capabilities that support legitimate business execution: CRM and demand qualification, RFQ and sourcing, buyer/supplier intelligence, pricing and economics, compliance and risk, logistics and shipping, documents, communications, finance, Cuba/private-sector operations, Sofía/AI operating support, authentication, audit/recovery, and deployment safeguards.

Legacy experiments, dead integrations, generated artifacts, duplicate deployment triggers, and obsolete runtime tooling should not be reintroduced without a current production workflow and owner-approved business case.
